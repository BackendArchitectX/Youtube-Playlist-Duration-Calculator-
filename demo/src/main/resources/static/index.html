Steps to find out YouTube Playlist Length

Here are the simple & easy steps to follow for calculating the YouTube Playlist Length with our tool.

1

Copy Your Playlist URL

Go to YouTube/YouTube Music, and find the playlist and copy its link. If you are on mobile app, click "Share" icon and click "Copy link" option. Now, the playlist link is copied.

Pro tip: Make sure the playlist is public or unlisted. If your Playlist is private, temporarily make it unlisted, after calculation, you can make it private again.
2

Enter Playlist URL

Once you are in our tool, you will find a input at the top of the website. Just paste the url you have copied.

3

Enter Start & End video numbers (Optional)

If you want to calculate the length between videos in Youtube Playlist, you can enter the start & end video numbers. This step is completely optional.

Use-case: If you are learning with a youtube playlist or listening to a music & assume the playlist has 27 videos/songs. You have watched 8 videos so far and want to calculate the length of remaining videos, you can enter the start and end video numbers for that scenario, you can enter the start and end numbers.
4

Click the Calculate button

Just click the "Calculate" button and you will get the YouTube Playlist length immediately. You can repeat this for any number of playlists, there is no restrictions.
